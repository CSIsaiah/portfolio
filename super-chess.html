My part in the project

From a new rule
to a usable interaction.

I worked with a team on a chess variant built around special piece abilities and anime-inspired piece designs. My responsibilities were the queen’s teleport mechanic and the popup menu used to activate it.

I connected the mouse interaction to the queen’s destination, position, and game state. To limit the ability’s power, the teleport routine prevents the queen from directly capturing the opposing king.

This page highlights my gameplay and interface work within the team project. The piece artwork is part of the shared game.
