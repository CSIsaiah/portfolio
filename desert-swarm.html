An individual project

One developer.
A connected set of systems.

I developed Desert Swarm individually, connecting movement and aiming, shooting, enemy behavior, spawning, health, experience, and the gameplay interface.

The core loop combines survival with progression: enemies become more frequent, the player unlocks weapons, and each weapon has its own level. The interface communicates health, experience, weapon information, and upgrades during play.

My contribution is the game’s design and programming. This page does not claim original creation of the supplied art or audio assets.

Single-player showcase
  1. 01

    Survive the swarm

    Move with WASD, aim with the mouse, and shoot with Space as enemy pressure increases.

  2. 02

    Unlock your weapons

    Begin with a pistol, then unlock a shotgun and rifle through combat progress.

  3. 03

    Switch and develop

    After all weapons unlock, switch with keys 1–3. Weapon levels improve firing speed or shotgun pellet count and spread.

Experimental · In development

I began adapting the game for two local players, adding separate movement and shooting controls, per-player health and experience tracking, and projectile ownership to identify which player defeated an enemy.

That extension is still unfinished. The single-player build remains the main playable showcase.

Current control limitation

Both characters face and shoot toward the same mouse cursor. Independent aiming is still unfinished.
