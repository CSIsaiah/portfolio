01 / Motivation

What needs care today?

Our starting point was someone who has a few houseplants but isn’t sure what each one needs or when to act. We wanted to make it easy to find a plant, check its care information, and see which tasks are still due.

02 / Online research

Learning from existing apps.

We compared existing plant apps and looked at published reviews. Gardenize made common plant-management actions take several steps. Seed to Spoon’s home screen felt crowded. Reviews of Plantum raised a different issue: unfinished reminders should stay visible.

That gave us three design priorities: make plants easy to reach, keep the home screen clear, and keep care tasks pending until the user marks them complete.

03 / Prototyping phase

From wireframes to an app.

In Figma, we explored layouts for browsing plants, scheduling care, adding plants, and following challenges. My wireframe work included Add Plant and Challenges. In the app, I contributed to Plant Search and Challenges.
